# Quiver build lab — 2026-10-31

The current goal is iterative testing before the Render cutover. Always-on hosting is not required for this release. Public preview AI is explicitly enabled with bounded accounting; record ownership still requires platform sign-in.

## Implemented

- Percentage-dip requests in Persian and English preserve the asset and percentage. Missing purchase amount and decline reference become editable fields, not invented trades. A 30% decline is a drawdown signal, not an absolute price threshold.
- Drawdowns can measure from peak since start/last fill, start/last fill, or a chosen lookback. One purchase per new drop event; unchanged quotes do not create repeated dip buys. Time-window indicators wait for sufficient observed history.
- Anonymous text/chart AI can use the existing configured OpenRouter provider. Atomic D1 accounting retains the global daily cap, adds a $1/day anonymous sub-cap and 30 provider calls/day per daily HMAC-pseudonymized network address. Raw IPs are not stored. Anonymous compiler access never authorizes online records or connected accounts. Set QUIVER_PREVIEW_PUBLIC_AI=false to end the public test allowance.
- A pure checkpointable workflow paper engine handles logical conditions, intervals/events, exact budgets plus modeled fees, leverage/margin, partial exits, realized-profit dependencies and outcome settlements supplied by the test data. Parallel stages cannot double-spend the same realized-profit pool. Existing legacy executor remains separate.
- The workflow lab runs explicit synthetic scenarios and tab-bound paper sessions using indicative market quotes. It shows stage statuses, fills, cash, equity and fees. A local checkpoint restores paused, with no fabricated catch-up across a closed tab.
- Read-only quotes use selected exact identities: Solana mint via DEX Screener, Hyperliquid mark/funding metadata, Polymarket outcome midpoint, and Tiingo underlying-stock quotes when the configured subscription allows them. No missing quote is replaced by synthetic data.
- Synthetic and quote sessions are visibly distinguished. Fees are modeled at 0.1% per fill; order-book execution, slippage, chain gas and perpetual funding charges are not modeled. Cross-venue funds in the lab are virtual.

## Keys and remaining activation

OpenRouter, Privy and Tiingo keys are already configured as runtime secrets. Public market metadata/quotes for Solana, Hyperliquid and Polymarket do not require a user's private signing key. Do not ask for private wallet keys just to build or paper test.

For tomorrow's Render activation: provision the prepared Node API/PostgreSQL/disk, wire existing secrets and permitted origins, bind the workflow driver to durable scheduling/checkpoints, rebuild the live-mode frontend and test ownership/import/recovery. Real order adapters, wallet signing grants, cross-chain fund movement and execution-cost calibration remain separate from paper testing. No real orders or paid Render resources were created in this build round.

The preview's continuous observations stop when its tab closes. Current-price sessions do not bootstrap historical candles, so indicators/lookbacks warm up from observed data. Live Polymarket midpoint data does not by itself prove resolution; resolved-profit behavior is tested with explicit scenario settlement events and still needs a verified production resolution feed before unattended use.
