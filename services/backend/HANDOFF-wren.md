**Read first:**
- `backend/api-contracts.md`: endpoints, SSE events, examples, mock mode.
- `backend/contracts/src/`: zod schemas and TS types. Import from `@quiver/contracts`, or copy into `web/lib/contracts/` until we set up a workspace.
- `backend/contracts/fixtures/`: a strategy plus a full paper-run event log that matches your `lib/mock.ts` scenario.

**What maps to your current code:**
- `Venue` = `"robinhood" | "solana" | "hyperliquid" | "polymarket"`, the same as `lib/mock.ts`.
- `NodeKind` watch/when/do/exit = signals/triggers/actions/exits. Node ids = strategy-local ids (`fed_odds`, `t1`, `a1`, `a1.exit`).
- `DRAFT_STEPS` → compile `progress` events (`step` + `label`). `partial` events stream graph nodes, so draw ghost nodes until `result`.
- `RULES` → `humanized` {watch, when, do, exit}, generated by the server. Render it verbatim and don't humanize client-side.
- `REFINE` chips → `POST /v1/compile` with `baseStrategy` + `instruction`.

**Run view:**
- Subscribe to `GET /v1/runs/:id/events/stream?ticket=…` with EventSource.
- Event effects: `tick` updates values and trigger states; `trigger_met` sets hot edges from `path` plus the hold countdown; `trigger_fired` makes the actions active; `fill` adds PnL and a tx link (live only); `error` shows a toast when retryable; `risk_halt` shows a persistent banner and Resume.
- `heartbeat` every 15 s. Show "Offline" after 45 s.

**Rules to follow:**
1. Money values are decimal strings. Format them and never do float math on them.
2. `value: null` or `stale: true` means "waiting for data", not 0.
3. Stock Tokens with `geoRestricted: true` stay visible but cannot be traded. Show why.
4. Polymarket is watch-only. Never offer a trade button for it.
5. Paper fills say "simulated". Every live fill links to Blockscout.
6. `mode_not_enabled` (403) is normal for testnet/live right now. Show "Coming after paper beta".
7. Backtest results always show `caveats` and `synthetic` badges.
